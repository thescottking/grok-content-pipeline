# Changelog

## 1.1 (Nov 17, 2026)
- Drop-in mode: put research or a topic in `_inbox/` (or message the Orchestrator) and the pipeline runs on its own.
- Automatic gates: humanizer score with rewrite on failure, checklist, fact-check that stops the run, WordPress verify.
- One final approval, then real-time publish (never scheduled). Approval mode is a setting.
- Generic `pipeline-voice` and `pipeline-brand` skills and the `content-pipeline-setup` first-run skill.
- 8 bot templates published on Grok Bot.
